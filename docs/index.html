Modules

One tool for the whole economy.

Built around how Mech Arena actually spends: credits and blueprints for levels, A-coins for ranks, and events that want both.

Live balance tracking

Credits and A-coins are read from the currency bar every 3 seconds. Every gain and spend is logged; misreads are detected and rolled back on their own.

Goal planner

Prioritised goals per currency, with progress, amount left and a projected completion date at your real pace.

Upgrade calculator

Mechs and weapons, every rarity, weapons by energy. Credits, blueprints and A-coins for any star and level range, checked against your resources.

Crate Rush analysis

Expected cost, median and 90th percentile, guarantee, rank of the prize and the upgrades it still needs, compared with buying it in the Gear Hub.

Mech sheet

Max HP, speed and weapon energy of 47 mechs, ranked against the whole roster, with ability, cooldown and community viability notes.

Spend guard

A no-spend streak, a daily mission budget that never breaks it, and a prompt you must answer when your balance drops outside a planned purchase.

Adaptive income rate

A 28-day weighted average of what you really earn per day, net of mission spends, with one-off rewards and days off handled correctly.

In-game overlay

Balances, goal progress and mission budget on top of the match. Draggable, three sizes, never steals focus.

Install

Running in under a minute.

For Mech Arena on Windows and macOS (Plarium Play) and on Linux through Proton. Nothing to install and nothing else to download: everything it needs is inside.

Download and extract

Put MechVault anywhere. Coming from the PowerShell version on Windows? Extract it into the same folder: accounts, goals and settings carry over.

Start it

Windows: MechVault.exe (if SmartScreen warns: More info › Run anyway). macOS: right-click MechVault.app › Open the first time, then allow Screen Recording. Linux: ./MechVault.

Open the hangar

MechVault reads your balances and starts tracking. From then on it starts with your computer.

Download for Windows macOS · Apple Silicon macOS · Intel Linux

macOS: needs macOS 12 or later; the app is not signed by Apple yet, hence the right-click › Open. Linux: automatic reading needs an X11 session and the tesseract-ocr package; on Wayland you enter balances by hand.

Release page

What it touches

Screen: only the Mech Arena window, like a screenshot, to read credits and A-coins. Internet: only the two Mech Arena community wikis (Fandom and wiki.gg), to update mech stats, and mechvault.github.io, to check for app updates (installed only if the SHA-256 fingerprint matches; can be turned off in Settings). Files: only its own data folder, plus a start-at-login entry that you can turn off. No admin rights, no game memory, no input automation, no account login.
